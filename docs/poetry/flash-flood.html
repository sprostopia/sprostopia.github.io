Flash Flood

You open up your heavens;
The group stands staring.
Your caverns have flooded.
You’re trapped.

Truth condenses warm and ripe
Trickling down your face
Loud, honest and sticky
As a drunk.

Your firefly eyes betray you;
They illuminate your cracks.
The flare set off –
The riot started –
Control surrendered to tear gas.

The group exudes an obligatory hug
Then retreats to a torrent of certainties:
‘It’ll be okay.’
‘It’s not your fault.’
You flush their flustered faces with rain.

That afternoon,
When the storm has cleared,
They’ll condemn your conceited affront.

Then unveil what they were thinking –
Amidst your attention-seeking –

Please stop crying,
cunt.
