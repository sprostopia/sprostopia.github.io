Jellyfish

I’ve learnt the ocean’s calm effuse,
How fury drains and spreads
To the clouding of the void’s subconscious,
United with the farewell dribble
of distant prey.

Nothing’s coming for me.

The darkness behind
cuts off the distant’s gaze.

Come close and it’s an accident –
my body insists upon it.

Come close and I’ll share you my pain.

you’re shocked.
now you’re distant.



I cannot be followed:
I have no direction.
I have no home to visit.
My limbs are claimed by the current’s clutch
Then thrown around,
change in a collector’s bucket.

But you say I’m the current,
Secluding a sea-seduced swimmer
until they sink,
stiff and salted
as shop-meat.

I cannot find my eyes
But you are my surrogate –
you see through cracked lenses,
your glasses are grimy and detatched.

So I see as a doll does,
stringy and stained,
left face-first in worked-up slime.



It’s almost my turn –
I can sense the sea’s shallows –
They reflect all the noise –
the echoed whispers swell to a harmony of venom.

I’m paralysed.
You draw me close –
the void shudders.

You give me a choice –

two.
only ever two.

Head down and be shredded to
curious fragments
by my bed’s eternal rocks.

Head up and get spread on the sands of the living:
Amorphous, ridiculed,
collapsed,
suffocating in the breath of your life
