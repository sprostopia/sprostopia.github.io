Sense

Blind me Gods,
I beg,
Refund my birthday gift!
I’m too small,
It doesn’t fit.
Sense is in surplus;
I wish no longer to think.

Me eyes keep opening,
Glue them shut!
Save me the burden of sight.
Everyone else,
Everything built,
It’s all too much;
My brain is swollen and popping.

My tears are trapped behind bulging eyeballs,
Please, give them the pleasure of bursting.

I’m scared sick,
I’m clogging my ears
With my fingers,
They’re clogging my ears
With their chat.
Harmless, harmful,
I’m all bunged up;
They’ve mixed together
Into one big clump,
Wedged through my fracturing skull.

You smell saturated;
You taste of collisions;
You touch like a surgeon’s incision;
Nerve upon nerve
I’m knotted –
Not braided –
I’ve learnt.
