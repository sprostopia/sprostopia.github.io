Canyon Swimming

The canyon embraces me:
I am ready for the plunge;

I step forward and my body pivots
About the foot
Better suited to land;

I am astounded
— it bends.
I have convinced it of my direction.
The adrenal ice of my granite foot has thawed
To a tiger cub,
Playful and ferocious,
Dragging me towards
(Not away from)
The hunt.

My foot leaves the boulder as I meander forward
And fly.
